THE CONSTRUCTION

Feynman’s construction, in order

  1. 01

    Equal area, equal time. Equal increments of mean anomaly produce equal-time wedges from the focus.

  2. 02

    Velocity changes. In an inverse-square field, the equal-time velocity-change vectors share a length and turning rule.

  3. 03

    Chain the changes. Equal-length vectors that turn by a constant angle close into the hodograph circle.

  4. 04

    Read the offset. Eccentricity displaces the hodograph’s center from the velocity origin.

  5. 05

    Return to position. The perpendicular relation between the hodograph radius and velocity recovers the conic orbit.

The circle-hodograph result predates Feynman and is associated with Hamilton (1846). Feynman’s elementary geometric presentation was reconstructed by David and Judith Goodstein in Feynman’s Lost Lecture: The Motion of Planets Around the Sun.
